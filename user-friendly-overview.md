## Overview

The StackSync AI Connector automatically keeps your settings up to date between appStack and Cloud2Code. When you make a change in appStack, this connector takes care of passing that change along to Cloud2Code — so you don't have to copy anything over by hand.

You will find and manage this feature inside **StackBuilder**, where it works quietly in the background to keep both systems in agreement.

One thing to keep in mind: syncing doesn't always happen instantly. Because it runs automatically in the background rather than on a fixed schedule, there can sometimes be a short delay before a change shows up on the other side.

## Set Up and Usage

Getting started with StackSync AI Connector doesn't require any special setup on your part — once it's turned on for your project, it works automatically.

1. **Make your changes as usual in appStack.** You don't need to do anything extra to start a sync - it's designed to happen on its own.
2. **Check on your sync anytime in StackBuilder.** This is where you can see whether your latest changes have come through. *(We are confirming exactly what status details will be shown here - this section will be updated soon.)*
3. **Want to trigger a sync yourself?** Look for the sync button. Right now it's called **Sync Now** - in an upcoming update, it will be renamed to **Run AI Sync**. Same button, same purpose, just a new name.

> A quick heads-up: if a sync doesn't seem to have gone through, give it a little time first - it may just be catching up in the background. If it still hasn't appeared after a while, see the Troubleshooting section below.
